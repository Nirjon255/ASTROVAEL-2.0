ASTROVAEL 26.0 - PROCEDURAL WORLDS, REAL SCALE CUES & MISSION CLOCK

Based on the Version 25.0 project. Route, navigation map, steering, boost, combat,
hazards, pickups, diamond wallet and save data (SaveData v2) are retained.

BUG FIXES
- Bolts now hit every hostile craft. Hit detection used the node name
  "HostileAlienCraft", but Godot renames duplicate sibling names, so every craft
  after the first was ignored. Hostiles are now identified by the "hostile_craft" group.
- A kill can no longer be awarded twice when two bolts hit the same craft in one frame.
- The velocity meter showed a unitless number while the notes said km/s. At the
  old scale the meter implied about 304,000 km/s, faster than light. It now shows
  simulated km/s: 29 at cruise, 47 with afterburner.
- Camera far clip (5,200) was shorter than Mars's distance, so Mars only appeared
  when the ship was almost there. Far clip is now 30,000 and Mars is visible as a
  small red disc from launch.
- Nav map Mercury closest approach corrected from 91.7M km to about 77M km.

REALISM
- Earth: new procedurally generated surface (oceans, continents, polar ice), a cloud
  layer, and a blue atmospheric limb. Earth's radius is kept at the real Earth:Mars
  ratio (about 1.89).
- Mars: replaced the old texture, which was a cyan ice map and looked grey. The new
  Mars is procedurally generated with rust-ochre dust, dark basalt plains, craters
  and small white polar caps. Axial tilt 25.2 degrees. The atmosphere is a faint
  butterscotch limb instead of a neon halo. Mars no longer has a cloud shell, because
  a shell that close to the surface z-fights at this distance.
- Moon: procedural grey regolith with dark maria and craters, tidally locked.
- Sun: the key light is now aimed from a single sun direction, and the sky draws
  the sun's glare at that direction. Terminators and lit faces agree. The light is
  white rather than tinted blue. Ambient light is lower to suit a space setting.
- Fog removed: space has no distance fog, so Mars and Earth keep their true colours.
- Asteroids are irregular procedural rocks with per-vertex tone, not stretched spheres.
- Velocity and time: 1 real second of flight equals 10,500 simulated seconds. The
  velocity meter, mission clock, ETA and one-way signal lag all use this one scale.
  Signal lag grows from 0 to about 3 min 02 s (54.6M km at light speed).
- Mission clock (MISSION T+) and simulated ETA replace the flight-unit readouts.
  Arrival shows the simulated arrival time.
- Note on time: a 54.6M km route at 29 km/s takes about 22 simulated days. A real
  Earth-Mars transfer takes roughly 6-9 months because its path is much longer and
  the drive is more modest. The 22-day figure is a stylised drive, and the nav map
  footnote says so.

ENVIRONMENT
- Departure: the ship starts with a 3.8 second opening in which the camera yaws
  back over the ship to show Earth and its Moon, then swings forward. Earth shrinks to
  a point as the ship climbs away.
- Star sky: a camera-following sphere with a spatial shader that draws stars in two
  densities, a Milky Way band with dust clouds, and a sun disc with glare. It is
  drawn behind all planets.
- Local near-field stars and the nebula backdrop are retained.

CONTROLS (unchanged)
- A / Left Arrow: steer left
- D / Right Arrow: steer right
- Space: boost
- F / FIRE: shoot
- NAV MAP: opens map, route and mission panel
- Escape: pause / resume
- R: restart after a run ends

TEST STATUS - PLEASE READ
- The four planet textures were generated and checked visually in this environment.
- The GDScript was patched and statically checked here: indentation, bracket balance,
  every called helper defined, every member declared. Godot 4.7 is NOT installed in
  this environment, so the project has NOT been compiled or run in the Godot editor.
- The APIs I am least certain of, in case the editor reports an error at these points:
  Basis.looking_at (key light orientation), SurfaceTool.commit (procedural rocks),
  FastNoiseLite.get_noise_3d, and the spatial star-sky shader (cull_front on the
  camera-following sphere).
- Keep Version 25.0 as a backup and send the first Godot error if one appears.
