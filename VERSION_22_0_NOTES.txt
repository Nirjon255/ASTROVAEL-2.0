ASTROVAEL 22.0 — ALIEN ENCOUNTER & SPACE COMBAT

NEW FEATURES
- Hold F on PC or hold the FIRE button on mobile to shoot twin energy bolts.
- Hostile alien spacecraft approach from ahead, travelling toward the player.
- Procedural alien fighter design with green energy cockpit and engine details.
- Projectile hits destroy hostile ships and trigger a short green energy burst.
- Each destroyed hostile awards 2 energy and increments the HOSTILES counter.
- Colliding with a hostile craft terminates the run.
- Enemy and projectile cleanup prevents objects accumulating indefinitely.
- Existing steering, boost, navigation map, journey display, hazards, pickups and save data are retained.

CONTROLS
PC: A/D or arrow keys to steer, SPACE to boost, F to fire, ESC to pause.
Mobile: left/right, BOOST and FIRE touch buttons. Hold FIRE for continuous shooting.

TECHNICAL NOTE
This project targets Godot 4.7 Compatibility renderer. The source and ZIP archive can be checked here,
but this environment does not have the Godot editor/runtime available, so engine compilation and gameplay
verification must be completed in the user's Godot installation.
